    Biography

  • Vishal Chourasia is a PhD researcher working under the supervision of Prof. Anirban Sengupta. His research interests include high-level synthesis (HLS), hardware IP protection, and hardware Trojan analysis for embedded and AI-driven systems. His work focuses on addressing emerging security challenges in CAD-driven and HLS-based hardware design flows, particularly for convolutional neural networks (CNNs), machine-learning accelerators and consumer-electronics IP cores.
  • His doctoral research has contributed to identity-driven watermarking and IP protection frameworks embedded at various stages of HLS. These approaches combine cryptographic primitives, biometric traits, and bio-inspired signatures to uniquely bind hardware designs to their legitimate owners, helping prevent IP piracy, overproduction, cloning, and malicious counterfeiting while maintaining design quality.
  • He has published in leading international journals, including IEEE Computer Society IT Professional, IEEE Embedded Systems Letters, IET Computers & Digital Techniques, and Nature Scientific Reports, and has presented his work at major IEEE conferences such as SOCC, ICCE-Taiwan, iSES, ICM, ICFTSS, and VDAT. His research contributes toward building secure and trustworthy hardware for next-generation AI systems.
